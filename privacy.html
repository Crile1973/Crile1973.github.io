YoutubeLibrary Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

YoutubeLibrary is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jason Gagne. It requests read-only access to the owner's own YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly).

What data it accesses

It reads the owner's playlists, playlist items, subscriptions and public video details such as titles, channels, durations and captions.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to organize and summarize the owner's own saved videos. It is stored privately on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Crile1973/Crile1973.github.io.
